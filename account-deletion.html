Spentee Account Deletion

Users of Spentee can permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete through the app

Open Spentee, go to Profile or Settings, select Delete Account, and follow the confirmation steps.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to spenteeapp@gmail.com using your registered account email address.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your Spentee account information.
  • Financial transactions and records.
  • Budgets, savings goals and categories.
  • Lending and borrowing records.
  • Associated cloud and local app data.

Account deletion is permanent. Data may be retained only where required by applicable law.
