Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5 2026

1. Introduction

Spentee (the "App") is a personal finance manager developed by the Spentee team. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up or sign in, we collect your Google account email and profile information (name, avatar) via Google Sign‑In, or the email/password you provide.
  • Financial data: Transactions, budgets, savings goals, debts, categories, and profile settings you create are stored locally and synchronized to Cloud Firestore.
  • Device identifiers: When AdMob is used, Google collects device identifiers for ad personalization as described in the AdMob policy.
  • AI query data: When you use the built‑in AI assistant, the query you type (and any referenced financial data) is sent to a self‑hosted backend service for processing.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you (Google Sign‑In or email/password).
  • To store and synchronize your financial data across devices via Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • To display personalized ads through Google AdMob (device identifiers are used by Google for ad targeting).
  • To generate AI responses based on the user query and, when needed, your financial data.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All user‑generated data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and on the device in a local JSON file (spentee_storage). Data remains stored until you delete your account using the app’s “Delete Account” feature, which removes all Firestore documents and the local storage. If you uninstall the app, the local JSON file is removed. No additional automatic expiration is applied.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties other than the services required for the app’s functionality (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and the self‑hosted AI backend).
  • Google may process data according to its own privacy policies for Firebase and AdMob.
  • The self‑hosted AI backend processes query data solely for generating responses and does not retain it beyond the request.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & correction: You can view and edit your financial data within the app.
  • Deletion: Use the “Delete Account” feature to permanently remove all your data from Firebase and the device.
  • Data export: You may request a copy of your stored data by contacting us.

7. Security

We rely on Firebase’s security rules (see firestore.rules) to restrict access to each user’s documents. All network communication uses HTTPS.

8. Third‑Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Core – for user management and data sync.
  • Google Sign‑In – for OAuth authentication.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) – for displaying ads.
  • Self‑hosted AI backend – for processing AI queries.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on the Play Store listing.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, please contact us at [spenteeapp@gmail.com](mailto:spenteeapp@gmail.com).
